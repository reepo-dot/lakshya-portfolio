About Lakshya

Driven by curiosity, fueled by physics, mechanisms, and design.

I am currently in my 1st Year of Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Mechanical Engineering. Ever since I first opened a bicycle gear mechanism as a child, I've been fascinated by how intricate machine assemblies transform basic energy into purposeful, graceful motion.

As a freshman engineering student, I combine foundational mechanical engineering principles—such as Statics, Dynamics, Thermodynamics, and Material Science—with modern software tools like CAD (AutoCAD & SolidWorks), Python, and rapid prototyping technologies.

My goal is to specialize in Automated Systems, Robotics, Advanced Machine Design, and Clean Energy Mobility. I actively participate in collegiate workshops, maker labs, and engineering design challenges.
